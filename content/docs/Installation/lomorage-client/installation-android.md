# Lomorage on Android: a new app is available

**This is a new, separate app, not an update.** "Lomorage: Free Up Photo Space" is a paid app: **$1.99, one time**. No subscription. The desktop app stays free.

**Why we're asking:** the old free Lomorage Android app is no longer maintained or updated. The new app is where Android development continues.

**You can keep using the old app.** Nothing is switched off by this notice, and you don't have to do anything.

[**Get the new app on Google Play**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wtao.lomo&referrer=utm_source%3Dlomo_app_dialog)

## What you get

- **Backup over home Wi-Fi**: photos and videos go from your phone to your own PC or Mac.
- **Free Up Space**: removes items from your phone that are already backed up.
- **On-device AI search**: find photos by content, including text in photos; browse on a map; find duplicates.

Photos go straight from your phone to your own computer, never through our servers.

## What about my existing photos?

Photos you already backed up stay on your own computer. The new app works with the Lomorage Photo Assistant you already run: it finds it on your home Wi-Fi (or you can type its address), and you sign in with your existing username and password. No new account and no new setup. Your existing library shows up in the app, and photos that are already backed up aren't uploaded again.

## Set up the computer side

The new app backs up to the free Lomorage Photo Assistant on your PC or Mac. If you haven't installed it yet:

- [Windows](/docs/Installation/lomorage-service/installation-win/)
- [Mac (OSX)](/docs/Installation/lomorage-service/installation-osx/)
- [Other platforms (Raspberry Pi, Linux, NAS, Docker)](/docs/Installation/lomorage-service/)

Your phone and computer need to be on the same home Wi-Fi.
